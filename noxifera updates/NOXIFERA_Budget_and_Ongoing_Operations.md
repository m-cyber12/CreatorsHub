# 35. What NOXIFERA Should NOT Hire For Too Early

Do not build a large team before usage proves the need.

Avoid early full-time hires for:

- large moderation team
- large content department
- sales team
- marketplace operations
- dedicated support department
- full research department
- DevOps-only role
- native mobile team

Use automation and targeted freelancers/contractors until workload justifies permanent staff.

---

# 36. The Operating Principle

The goal is NOT to create a company where people manually maintain every page.

Build:

```text
AUTOMATION
   ↓
Collect
   ↓
Detect
   ↓
Draft
   ↓
Test
   ↓
AI processing
   ↓
Human QA
   ↓
Trusted publication
```

Automate repetitive work.

Keep humans on:

- trust;
- methodology;
- research;
- moderation;
- editorial judgment;
- partnerships;
- high-value support.

## Several-thousand-DAU scale

Possible expansion:
- 2–4 engineers

- 1 community manager + moderators
- 1 growth person
- 1 support person

Staff according to workload, not vanity metrics.

---

# 38. Final Rule

> **Spend money to increase truth, utility and retention — not merely feature count.**

The best early investments are the ones that make NOXIFERA:

- more accurate;
- more useful;
- more trustworthy;
- more personalized;
- more executable.

A larger team should come only when the product has enough usage that manual work, support, research or infrastructure has become the bottleneck.
